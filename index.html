About

About Ritika Raje

I spend my time on the messy middle of delivery — the part where strategy meets a real operation with real people, real systems, and real constraints, and someone has to make it actually work.

Over nine years I've done that across three very different worlds: robotics and warehouse automation, high-voltage energy transmission, and regulated pharmaceutical manufacturing. Different industries, same job — find where the operation is losing time or money, redesign it, and make the improvement hold after I move on.

More recently I've been building AI into that work directly. I've designed and shipped my own applications end to end, so when I talk about AI in operations it comes from having built the thing, not from a deck.

I also lead programme and conference delivery for the PMI UK Chapter as a volunteer — most recently directing a global sustainability summit.
